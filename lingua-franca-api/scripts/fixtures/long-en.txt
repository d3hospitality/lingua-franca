In recent years the hospitality industry has changed in ways nobody expected. Restaurants now rely on technology for reservations, ordering, and even pairing wine with food. When I first started cooking professionally in Lisbon, everything was done by hand and from memory. Today a young chef carries a tablet, checks inventory in real time, and adjusts the menu based on what sells. But the heart of the work has not changed at all. You still need to taste constantly, respect your ingredients, and cook for the person sitting at the table, not for a camera. That is something no machine will ever replace, no matter how advanced the kitchen becomes over the next decade.
